# Akhila Rendale Portfolio — Easy Editing Guide

You can edit this portfolio anytime without asking anyone to change it.

## 1. Change your profile photo

Go to:

`assets/images/profile.jpg`

Replace that file with your new photo and keep the **same filename: `profile.jpg`**.

Then open `index.html` again. No HTML/CSS changes are needed.

### If your new photo is PNG/JPEG
For the easiest replacement, save/rename your new image as:

`profile.jpg`

## 2. Change your resume

Replace:

`assets/resume/Akhila_Rendale_Resume.pdf`

Keep the same filename. The View Resume and Download Resume buttons will continue to work.

## 3. Change certificates/documents

The files are inside:

`assets/certificates/`

You can replace a certificate while keeping its existing filename, or edit `index.html` if you want to add/remove a document.

## 4. Edit your portfolio text

Open:

`index.html`

You can edit your:
- About section
- Skills
- Experience
- Projects
- Education
- Certificate names/descriptions
- Contact information

You do not need to change `style.css` unless you want to change the design.

## 5. Open the portfolio

Double-click:

`index.html`

It will open in your browser.

## Recommended tools

For easy editing, use **Visual Studio Code**. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Code**.

## Important

Keep the folder structure unchanged. In particular, keep these folders:

- `assets/images/`
- `assets/resume/`
- `assets/certificates/`

This makes it easy to replace your photo, resume, and documents without changing the HTML.
